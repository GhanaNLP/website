GhanaNLP conducts research in Natural Language Processing

We build the datasets, models and benchmarks that Ghanaian languages are missing, and we publish what we find. The work is open: our corpora, models and evaluation code are released for anyone to build on, and the research is done by the community itself.

NSANKU: EVALUATING ZERO-SHOT TRANSLATION PERFORMANCE OF LLMS FOR GHANAIAN LANGUAGES

Our most recent paper benchmarks 19 open-weight and proprietary large language models translating between English and 43 Ghanaian languages — the most comprehensive evaluation of its kind so far. The headline finding is a sobering one: no model and no language reached both high performance and high consistency, so these systems are not yet reliably usable for Ghanaian language translation at scale. The benchmark is public and community-extensible. Read the paper on arXiv

GHANA NATIONAL AI STRATEGY LAUNCH

We attended the launch of Ghana's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy, alongside government, industry and academic stakeholders shaping how AI is built and applied in Ghana. Our interest there is the one we have carried throughout: that Ghanaian languages are part of the foundation, so the systems built on this strategy are usable by everyone in the country.
