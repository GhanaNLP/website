## What is Nsanku?

Nsanku evaluates how well Large Language Models translate Ghanaian languages. We tested **14+ models** across **43 languages** with 300+ sentences each.

## Why It Matters

Developers need to know which LLM works best for their language. Nsanku provides that evidence with BLEU and chrF scores.

## Models Evaluated

- **Closed**: GPT-4.1, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Flash
- **Open**: Llama 3.1/3.3/4, Qwen3 235B, DeepSeek V3.1, and more

## Languages

43 Ghanaian languages including Twi, Ewe, Fante, Ga, Dagbani, Hausa, and lesser-studied ones.
