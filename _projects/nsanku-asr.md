## What is Nsanku ASR?

Nsanku ASR benchmarks speech recognition models on Ghanaian languages. We test organization-owned models across **43 languages** using our [ghana-speech-eval](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ghananlpcommunity/ghana-speech-eval) dataset.

## How It Works

1. **Discover**: Scrape HuggingFace for ASR models per language
2. **Filter**: Keep only organization-owned models
3. **Evaluate**: Score each model on WER/CER across categories

## Results

Published per-language with category breakdowns (Bible, JW, Finance, UNICEF). Interactive leaderboard shows global rankings.
